Acting Justly,
Loving Mercy &
Walking Humbly with God
Brandon Hatmaker · Micah 6:8
✦ Key Verse
"He has shown you, O mortal, what is good. And what does the Lord require of you? To act justly and to love mercy and to walk humbly with your God."
— Micah 6:8
Understand the Context

Is the sum of your faith more than simply attending church, studying the Bible and socialising with your Christian friends? One of the responsibilities of God's church is to reach out to the poor and needy.

Contrary to popular belief, serving the least does not make a missional church. It is about serving the least and your neighbour. It's about balancing the fasting and the feast — making the altar both a place for communion and a place to leave your shoes.

"Each of us has the responsibility to navigate what serving the least looks like in our life, in our context, and in our church."

Missional describes the sending or incarnational efforts of the church — to "put on" Jesus, to represent him by focusing on being his hands and feet to our world. Attractional refers to programmes and events designed to gather people to the church campus.

These two are not opposing forces. Their beauty is found in their coexistence: working in perfect harmony by taking the focus off ourselves and placing it where it ought to be — on God and on others.

What This Means

Mercy is immediate — it is showing kindness and compassion right now, to the person in front of you in immediate need.

Justice goes further — it is fighting for systems and situations to be made right for everyone, especially the poor, the marginalised, and the oppressed.

Going to church is the beginning, not the whole, of your faith.
